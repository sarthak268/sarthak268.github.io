<section class="home-section" aria-labelledby="background-title">
    <div class="section-heading">
        <span class="section-index" aria-hidden="true">01 / BACKGROUND</span>
        <h2 id="background-title">A little about me</h2>
    </div>
    <div class="section-body home-bio">
        <p>I'm the first hire at <a href="https://warp.xyz">Warp Sciences</a>, co-leading the robot learning effort. I'm building robots to automate scientific discovery, starting with small molecules.</p>
        <p>Previously, I was a research engineer at <a href="https://www.1x.tech/">1X Technologies</a>, contributing to <a href="https://www.1x.tech/discover/redwood-mobility">Redwood AI</a> and the <a href="https://www.1x.tech/discover/world-model-self-learning">1X World Model</a>. Before that, I was the first hire and founding research engineer at <a href="https://www.generalrobotics.company/">General Robotics</a>. I led sim2real reinforcement learning and technical business development, working across humanoids, quadrupeds, and manipulator arms. I hold an MS in Robotics from Carnegie Mellon University.</p>
        <details class="earlier-chapters">
            <summary>The earlier chapters</summary>
            <p>My first taste of startup life was at <a href="https://preimage.ai/">Preimage</a>, working on 3D reconstruction. Before that, I was a visiting researcher at the <a href="https://www.clvrai.com/">CLVR Lab</a>, USC, working on continual skill learning, and a research assistant at <a href="https://declare-lab.github.io/">SUTD</a>, working on vision language models.</p>
            <p>My path into AI started during my B.Tech (with honors) at <a href="https://www.iiitd.ac.in/">IIIT-Delhi</a>. I got my first taste of robotics with the <a href="https://sites.google.com/iiitd.ac.in/iiitd-alive/home">ALIVE</a> autonomous driving team, interned at the <a href="https://sutdbrain.wordpress.com/about/">SUTD Brain Lab</a> working on representation learning and at <a href="https://www.eng.nus.edu.sg/bme/staff/ren-hongliang/">NUS</a> working on medical robotics, and spent much of my undergrad buried in research.</p>
        </details>
